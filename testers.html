Become a Purrfolio tester

Help me test the Android app before it launches on Google Play.

Google requires new apps to be tested by at least 12 people for 14 days before launch. If you have an Android phone, you can help in three quick steps. No emails to me, no forms. Joining the group below gives you access automatically.

How to join

  1. Join the tester group with the Google account you use on the Play Store.
    Join the Google Group
  2. Become a tester. Open the testing link on your Android phone (or in a browser signed in to the same Google account) and tap Become a tester. If it says the page isn't available, wait a few minutes and try again.
    Open the testing link
  3. Install Purrfolio from the Play Store link on that page, then use it normally for the next two weeks.
⏳
Please stay opted in for 14 days. Google counts testers who remain enrolled for two weeks, so don't leave the testing programme or uninstall the app until I tell you the test is over. Opening the app now and then helps too.

Questions

Do I need a specific Google account?

Yes. Use the same Google account that is signed in to the Play Store on your Android phone, for both the group and the testing link.

Is it free? Will it cost me anything?

Completely free. Testing doesn't involve any payment.

What happens to my data?

The app works as described in our Privacy Policy. You can delete your account and data at any time, see how.

I found a bug or have feedback.

Thank you! Email support@purrfolio.app or drop by our Discord.

I don't have an Android phone.

Sadly closed testing is Android only, but you can still try the web app and share this page with an Android-using friend.
