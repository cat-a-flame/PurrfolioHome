🐾Getting started

How do I create an account?

Open Purrfolio, tap Sign up and enter your email and a password. Check your inbox for a confirmation email and tap the link to activate your account.

How do I add my first account?

Go to Settings → Accounts and tap Add account. Choose a type (bank, cash, savings, credit card, investment, bond, crypto…), a currency and a starting balance.

How do I record an expense?

Tap the + button, choose Expense, Income or Transfer, fill in the amount, account and category, and save. You can add notes, a payer and labels too.

🧶Using Purrfolio

How do planned (recurring) payments work?

Open Planned payments and add a payment with its amount, account, category and frequency. When a payment is due, Purrfolio highlights it so you can confirm it with one tap. It's then logged as a real transaction.

How do I import transactions from a CSV file?

Go to Settings → Import and choose your CSV file. Purrfolio detects the delimiter, date and amount formats. You then map columns, accounts and categories and preview the rows before importing. The file is processed on your device.

Why don't my totals match my bank exactly?

Check that each account's starting balance is correct and that all transactions (including transfers) are recorded. Totals in other currencies are converted using daily reference exchange rates, which can differ slightly from your bank's rates.

How do I hide my balances?

On the Dashboard, tap the eye icon next to the title to hide or show all amounts.

🐟Cost & tips

Does Purrfolio cost anything?

No. Purrfolio is free, with no subscriptions and no paid features. If you'd like to support it, you can leave a tip. Tips are a thank-you only: they don't unlock features or add anything extra.

Can I get a tip refunded?

Tips are processed by Google Play, so refunds are requested through Google Play under its refund policy. If you run into trouble, email us with your order number (it starts with GPA.) and we'll help.

🔒Account & privacy

How do I delete my account?

Open your profile settings in the app, tap Delete account and confirm. Can't get into the app? The account deletion page explains the other options.

How is my data handled?

Your data is encrypted in transit, only you can access your records, and it's never sold or used for ads. The Privacy Policy has all the details.

🧰Troubleshooting

I didn't receive the confirmation email.

Check your spam folder, then wait a few minutes and try again. Still nothing? Email us.

I forgot my password.

Email us from your registered address and we'll help you get back in.

My data isn't showing up on another device.

Make sure you're signed in with the same email on both devices and that you're online, then pull to refresh or restart the app.

The app looks broken or won't load.

Update to the latest version from Google Play and restart the app. If it still happens, let us know below.

Still stuck?

Email support@purrfolio.app for bugs and help with the app, or meow@purrfolio.app for general questions and ideas. You can also find us on Discord. We usually reply within 1–2 business days. Please never send us your password.
