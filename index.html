A peek at what's ahead

Pick a period and Purrfolio estimates the expenses and income you can expect in it. It looks at your last six months, category by category, and shows how sure it is about each estimate. It's a guess, not a promise, but it helps you see a tight month coming.

  • Expected expenses and income for the selected period
  • Learned from your own history, not generic averages
  • A confidence score and typical range for every estimate

FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Is Purrfolio free?

Yes. Purrfolio is free to use, with no subscriptions and no paid features. If you'd like to support it, you can leave a tip. A tip is just a thank-you: it doesn't unlock anything or add extra features.

Does Purrfolio connect to my bank?

No, and it never will. Connecting to banks brings real security risks, and Purrfolio is a hobby project built by one person, so bank connections are deliberately left out. You add transactions yourself or import a CSV file you've exported, so you decide what goes in. More about Purrfolio.

Which currencies are supported?

Accounts can currently be held in Euro (EUR), US Dollar (USD) and Hungarian Forint (HUF). Totals are converted using up-to-date reference exchange rates. More currencies are on the way.

Is Purrfolio available on iPhone?

Not at the moment. Purrfolio is built by one person, and there isn't capacity to build and maintain an iOS app right now. On an iPhone or iPad you can still use the web app in your browser.

Can I use Purrfolio on more than one device?

Yes. Your data is securely synced to your account, so you can sign in on your phone or in any browser at my.purrfolio.app and see the same numbers everywhere.

How do I import my existing data?

Export your transactions from your bank or previous app as a CSV file, then open Settings → Import. Purrfolio detects the format, lets you map columns to accounts, categories and labels, and previews everything before importing.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time, right in the app: open your profile settings, tap Delete account and confirm. Can't access the app? See our account deletion page for step-by-step instructions.

Is my data safe?

All traffic is encrypted with HTTPS, and our database enforces row-level security so each user can only ever access their own records. We never sell or share your data for advertising.

Is Purrfolio made with AI?

Purrfolio is built by one person, and I use AI tools to help me write the code. That's how one person can run a project like this. Every change goes through me before it ships. Your data is never exposed to AI: your records, accounts, email address and everything else you store in Purrfolio are not sent to any AI service, and they never will be. Read more about how Purrfolio is made.

Do I need a cat to use Purrfolio?

Not at all. Purrfolio works just as well for dog people, plant people and people people. Fair warning, though: if you do have a cat, it will probably sit on your keyboard while you log expenses.
