About

Made by one person, with care.

Purrfolio is a hobby project, built and looked after by me, Stefanie.

Why Purrfolio exists

I tried a lot of budgeting and expense apps. Some were hard to use, some were expensive, and none of them covered everything I needed. So I started building my own.

A team of one (with some AI help)

Everything — the design, the code, the support emails — is done by me, alone. I use AI tools to help me design and write code faster, which is what makes a project like this possible for a single person. Every change still goes through me before it reaches you.

Built with

In the spirit of transparency, these are the tools and services behind Purrfolio:

  • Supabase — accounts, database and backend.
  • Netlify — hosts the web app and this website.
  • Bitrise — builds the Android app.
  • GitHub — hosts the source code.
  • Claude — the AI assistant that helps me design and write code.

Services that handle your data are listed in the Privacy Policy.

Made for me, so I care

Purrfolio is a hobby, but it's also the app I use for my own money every day. That's why I care about the details: how quickly you can log an expense, whether the numbers add up, and whether the app is actually pleasant to open. If something feels off, I notice it too.

What Purrfolio won't do

Purrfolio will never connect to your bank. Linking bank accounts brings real security risks, and as a one-person project I don't want to hold the keys to anyone's bank. You add transactions yourself, or import a CSV file you've exported from your bank or another app.

  • No ads, and no selling or renting your data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking.

Say hello

Bug reports, feature ideas or just a hello are all welcome at purrfolioapp@proton.me. As it's just me, replies can take a day or two — but I read every message.
